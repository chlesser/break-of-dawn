### All In
Cost: Primary Operation, Secondary Operation, Tertiary Operation, All Movement (at least 5 ft) 
Effect: When taking the All In maneuver, you expend all your movement speed towards 1 target, must end your turn within 5 ft of them, and make all of your attacks at that 1 target. The Dodge DC of your attacks are increased by \+15, and all attacks that hit gain an extra damage die.
